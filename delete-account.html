Delete Your Koda Account

Last updated: July 21, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Koda account and the data associated with it.

Option 1 — Delete from within the App (recommended)

  1. Open the Koda app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the request. Your account and data will be permanently deleted.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, you can request deletion by email:

Send an email to info@easyhookai.space from the email address associated with your Koda account, with the subject line "Delete my Koda account". We will process your request and confirm once completed.

What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your profile and account information
  • All your memories (notes, photos, documents, voice recordings)
  • Your subject profiles
  • Your calendar events
  • All uploaded files (photos, documents, avatars) stored on our servers
  • Your authentication record
This action is irreversible. Once your account and data are deleted, they cannot be recovered. If you belong to a shared space that has no other members, that space and its shared content are also removed.

Contact

For any questions about account or data deletion, contact us at info@easyhookai.space.
