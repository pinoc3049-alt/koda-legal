Privacy Policy for Koda

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Koda ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Koda mobile application (the "App"), a family memory diary that lets you record memories, notes, photos, voice notes, first times and events, and ask an AI assistant questions about the content you have saved. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

Koda is a diary for families. You, the account holder, decide what content to add. You are solely responsible for the content you choose to store, including any information, photos, or documents relating to other people (such as your own children or family members). By adding such content you confirm that you are entitled to do so and that you have the authority and any necessary consent to store it.

Who Is Responsible for Your Data

The data controller is D.B., the developer and operator of Koda, established in Poland (European Union). You can reach us about anything in this policy at info@easyhookai.space.

Information We Collect

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our App for you:

  • Account Data: When you create an account (via email or Google Sign-In), we collect:
    • Email address
    • Display name
    • Profile photo (optional)
  • Content You Create ("Memories"): We store the content you voluntarily add to your diary, including:
    • Text notes
    • Photos and documents you upload
    • Voice notes: your speech is turned into text by the speech recognition service of your device (provided by Google on Android or Apple on iOS), and Koda stores the resulting text. Koda does not store the audio.
    • "First times" (milestones) with the date you choose
    • Calendar events and reminders
  • Subject Profiles: You may create profiles for the people or pets your diary is about (a "subject"), which can include a name, date of birth, notes, and optional details such as weight or height. This information is entered by you, at your discretion, for your own organizational purposes.
  • Family / Shared Space Data: Koda allows you to invite other people to a shared space so that memories can be viewed together. If you send an invitation, we process the invitee's email address in order to deliver the invitation. Members of the same shared space can see the memories, subjects, and events within that space.
  • AI Interaction Data: When you use the AI assistant, your question, the recent messages of that chat and the content of your diary (your saved memories, subject profiles, first times and upcoming events) are processed to generate a personalized answer. When you upload a photo or a document, it is processed to create a description or to extract its text. See "Artificial Intelligence and Your Consent" and "Third-Party Services" below.
  • Your AI choice: We store whether you agreed to the AI features, and the date of your last choice, with your account.
  • Notifications: Reminders and the optional "question of the day" are scheduled on your device. They are not sent through our servers.

Artificial Intelligence and Your Consent

  • The AI features are optional and are switched off until you agree. The App asks for your consent the first time you ask the AI a question or upload a photo or a document.
  • If you agree, the content described above is sent to Google's Gemini API to produce the answer, the photo description or the extracted text.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time in Profile > AI Authorization. After that, nothing more is sent to the AI provider. Text notes, voice notes, first times and the agenda keep working without it.
  • If you share a space with your family, a question asked by any member who has agreed is answered using the memories of the whole shared space.

Use of Data

Koda uses the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the App and your family diary
  • To enable the AI assistant to answer questions about your saved content
  • To enable shared-space (family) features you choose to use
  • To send invitation emails when you invite someone to your space
  • To provide customer support
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues and abuse
  • To process subscriptions and payments via our providers

Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may process information on our behalf:

  • Supabase: For authentication, database hosting (with Row Level Security enabled), and file storage.
  • Google (Gemini API): To power the AI assistant, photo captioning, and document text extraction. The content relevant to your request is sent to Google's Gemini API for processing so that a response can be generated.
  • Google Sign-In: Used, optionally, to let you sign in with your Google account.
  • Resend: Used to deliver invitation emails to addresses you choose to invite.
  • RevenueCat / Google Play Billing: For managing in-app subscriptions. These providers process your purchase history and anonymous identifiers to validate transactions.

Each third-party service operates under its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review their policies.

Legal Basis

  • Performance of our contract with you (the Terms of Service): to create your account, store and show your diary, share it with the people you invite, and manage your subscription.
  • Your consent: to send your content to the AI provider. You can withdraw it at any time, as described above.
  • Our legitimate interest: to keep the App secure and to prevent abuse.

Where Your Data Is Processed

Your diary is stored by Supabase on servers located in the European Union (Ireland). Some of our providers (Google, Resend, RevenueCat) are based in the United States or may process data there. Where personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, the transfer relies on the safeguards provided for by the GDPR, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers provided by Supabase. We implement industry-standard security measures, including Row Level Security (RLS), so that users can only access data belonging to their own account or shared space (the AI assistant is restricted in the same way), and private storage buckets with signed URLs for photos and documents. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide you with the App's services. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In-App: Use the "Delete Account" feature within the Profile / Settings page.
  • Web Portal: If you cannot access the app, you can initiate a deletion request at: https://pinoc3049-alt.github.io/koda-legal/delete-account.html
  • Upon deletion, your profile, memories, subjects, events, and uploaded files will be permanently removed. This action is irreversible.

Content About Other People and Children

Koda does not target children and is intended to be used by adults (see "Age Requirement" below). Koda does not knowingly collect data directly from children. Where an account holder chooses to record information about another person — including their own child — that content is provided by, and under the control and responsibility of, the account holder acting as the party who determines what is stored. If you believe someone has stored information about you or your child without authorization, please contact us using the details below and we will address the request.

Age Requirement

You must be at least 16 years of age (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country) to create an account and use the App. We do not knowingly allow children under this age to register.

AI Content and Safety

  • AI-generated responses are provided for informational and personal-organization purposes only and may contain errors or inaccuracies.
  • The AI assistant does not provide medical, legal, or professional advice.
  • You should exercise your own judgment and not rely solely on AI output for important decisions.

Your Rights (GDPR)

As Koda is operated from within the European Union, and depending on your location, you have certain data protection rights, including the right to access, rectify, export, restrict or object to the processing of your data, to request its deletion, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the processing carried out before. To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email address below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in particular the one of the country where you live; in Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).
